About

David Gorman

Cybersecurity student at Swinburne University in Melbourne, graduating in 2027. I'm interested in every side of security: offensive, defensive, forensics and OSINT. The deeper I dig, the more I find I enjoy.

01Background

I discovered cybersecurity in 2022 through The Cyber Mentor on YouTube and was hooked almost immediately. After working through his long-form ethical hacking and penetration testing courses, I knew this was what I wanted to do, so I created a Hack The Box account and started learning hands-on.

Next I enrolled at TAFE, which introduced me to the fundamentals: networking, building virtual machines, SIEMs and Windows Active Directory. That's where I came to appreciate the defensive side, and how all the pieces of an environment actually fit together.

I was then accepted into Swinburne and moved to Melbourne. University gave me a much stronger foundation in networking and protocols, along with programming and scripting in Python, Ruby, C#, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and assembly. I've studied IT security more broadly, from state-sponsored threat actors and industry standards to principles like Zero Trust architecture, and learned to write technical papers and reports. It also pushed me outside my comfort zone, presenting on both technical and non-technical topics and working in teams on technical projects with tools like Git.

Outside of uni I keep working through Hack The Box machines and building my own infrastructure. I run an Oracle Cloud web server (the one hosting this site), Pi-hole for network-wide DNS filtering, and Tailscale to connect my devices securely, with a focus on getting the security fundamentals right, from hardening to TLS certificates.

Throughout all of this I've worked in hospitality, first as a bartender and now as a Team Leader.
